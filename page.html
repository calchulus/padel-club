    Calibrate with real booking data

    Paste hourly occupancy from your own booking export, or from sampling competitors' public booking grids, and the model reshapes its hourly demand and rescales its overall level to match.

    One row per hour: daytype,hour,occupancy where daytype is wd (weekday) or we (weekend), hour is 6–23 and occupancy is 0–1 (or a percentage). Matched against month 12 of the current run.
